Quiz Board games

Paste a whole game

Paste all your categories, clues and answers at once. Simple text or JSON both work. You can also copy the AI prompt, ask ChatGPT for a game, and paste its answer here.

What can I paste?

Simple text. A Title: line, then a Category: line before each group of clues. Write each clue as clue = answer (a | or a tab also works). Clues go from easiest to hardest.

Title: Unit 3 Review
Points: 100, 200, 300, 400, 500

Category: Jobs
He works in a hospital. = a doctor
She teaches children. = a teacher

You can also write Q: clue on one line and A: answer on the next.

JSON. The text from "Copy game text" in the editor, or any JSON like this:

{"title": "Unit 3 Review",
 "categories": [
  {"name": "Jobs", "clues": [
   {"q": "He works in a hospital.", "a": "a doctor"},
   ["She teaches children.", "a teacher"]]}]}

Up to 6 categories and 6 clues in each.

Word Bomb

Pass it before it explodes

How to play
  1. You see a topic, for example Things in a kitchen.
  2. The team with the bomb says one English word for the topic.
  3. The teacher presses Good word, pass! and the bomb goes to the next team.
  4. Nobody knows when it explodes. The team holding it loses a heart.
  5. The last team with hearts wins.

Word Scramble

Fix the mixed-up word

How to play
  1. The letters of an English word are mixed up, for example T A C.
  2. Teams read the clue and try to find the word: CAT!
  3. The first team to say it and spell it gets the points.
  4. Too hard? Press Hint and one letter goes in its place, but the word is worth 1 point less.
  5. The team with the most points wins.
